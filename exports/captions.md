## Posting notes

- **Privacy:** every face is blurred, kids and staff, tracked through each clip frame by frame and checked by eye. Children's names on their paper plates and on the classroom wall are blurred too.
- **Filming dates:** going by the clips' own dates, the paint was filmed on November 3 and the marshmallow towers on November 4. The reel doesn't state a date, but keep it in mind if you caption it as all one day.
- **All real footage:** apart from the graphics and the paint transitions, everything is your own video and photos, with no AI-generated scenes.
- **Music:** "Cinematic Corporate" by alex-morgan, from Pixabay, free for commercial use. It wasn't flagged for Content ID on its Pixabay listing when checked on November 4, 2026. A credit is optional: `🎵 Music: "Cinematic Corporate" by alex-morgan (Pixabay)`.
- **Upload quality:** apps shrink videos unless told not to:
  - **Instagram:** turn on "Upload at highest quality" before posting (Settings and activity > Data usage and media quality).
  - **Facebook:** set video upload quality to HD under Settings > Media.
  - **TikTok:** switch on "Allow high-quality uploads" under More options on the post screen.
  - **Getting the file onto the phone:** don't send it through WhatsApp or Messenger, which re-compress videos. Use Google Drive, AirDrop or a USB cable.
- **Cover:** on Instagram, tap "Edit cover" and upload `nothing_cover.jpg`.
