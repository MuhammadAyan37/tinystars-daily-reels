# New-family offer: picture ad, copy and campaign plan

A picture ad for Tiny Stars Daycare. The main photo is the stained-glass classroom. Over it sits a printed coupon with a tear-off edge: **$100 off your first 2 months + free registration**. Below that is a parent's 5-star words from Google, three small real photos (indoor play gym, painting, turf play yard) and a **Book a tour** button with the phone number.

## What I looked at first

- **Kita Academy** (@kita_academy_ca): their posts with the most views were simple offer graphics over a room photo, for example "Grand Opening" (19.1K views) and "Free registration till 10 July" (14.3K). They had only 5 to 14 likes each, so those views came from paid reach. A clear offer works when money is behind it.
- **Little Penguins** (@littlepenguinsdaycare): a calm, professionally filmed reel of the space (7.4K views, 86 likes), and an ad made from a grid of real photos with one line of text and a waitlist button. It looks premium, but it has no offer.
- **Most other daycare posts** in the "Grande Prairie daycare" search are loud, AI-made flyers with cartoon children. Real photos stand out against them.

This ad takes the clear offer from Kita and the real, calm photography from Little Penguins, and adds social proof from a real review. None of them have all three.

## Campaign plan for $900 to $2,000, aiming for 10 enrolments

**How 10 enrolments add up.** These are rough planning numbers, not a promise; check them against your own results after the first week.
- About half the parents who fill in the form book a tour, and about half of the tours enrol.
- So 10 enrolments need about 20 tours, which needs about 40 to 50 leads.
- Local childcare leads on Meta often cost somewhere between $15 and $35 each. At that price, $900 to $1,500 buys about 40 to 60 leads.

**Setup**
- **Objective:** Leads, with an Instant Form set to "Higher intent" (people review their answers before sending, so you get fewer fake leads).
- **Form questions:**
  - name and phone (pre-filled)
  - your child's age: 12–19 months / 19 months–3 / 3–5 / 5–6 / 6–12
  - when you'd like to start: this month / next month / later
  - the best time for a tour
- **Form intro:** "New families get $100 off their first 2 months, plus free registration. Tell us a little about your child and we'll call you to book a tour."
- **Form privacy link:** Meta asks for a privacy policy link on the form. If tinystars.ca doesn't have a privacy page yet, it needs one before the form can go live.
- **Location:** a 15 km radius around 10629 West Side Drive (Grande Prairie and the edge of the county).
- **Audience:** ages 22 to 45. Leave Advantage+ audience on so Meta can find parents.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements, with **Customize placements**: the 4:5 picture for Feeds, the 9:16 picture for Stories and Reels.
- **Budget:** start at about $35 a day with option A and option B in the same ad set. After 7 days, keep the one with the cheaper cost per lead and turn the other off. Raise the budget by about 20% every few days while the cost per lead holds. Over 30 days that spends $1,000 to $1,500, with the rest of the $2,000 kept for scaling what works.

**What decides whether you get the 10**
- **Call every lead within 5 minutes during the day.** Leads go cold within hours, and fast follow-up makes the biggest difference of anything here.
- **Keep a simple sheet:** lead, called, tour booked, tour done, enrolled. After two weeks you'll know your real cost per enrolment.
- **Offer a tour time on the first call** rather than "come by any time".

## Before the ad goes live

- **Pin down the offer wording:** is it $100 off each of the first 2 months ($200 in total) or $100 off in total? Who counts as a new family? Is there an end date? A deadline ("enrol by November 30") usually brings more people in. State the terms in the form intro, and in the primary text if the deadline is short.
- **Alberta's affordability grant:** if Tiny Stars is in the provincial flat-fee program, check that a parent-fee discount fits its rules.
- **The review quote:** it's public on Google and shown without a name. It's still kind to ask that parent if you can use their words in an ad.
- **Photos:** all from the Tiny Stars Google page and your Drive. Nobody's face is in any of them, the child's name on the painting is blurred, and a face scan of all three finished pictures found no faces.
- **Phone:** make sure (780) 230-1599 is answered while the ad runs, including the first evenings.
