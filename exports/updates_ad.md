# "While you're at work": ad copy and setup notes

A 25-second paid ad for Tiny Stars Daycare. It opens on a teacher's hand swooping in for a high five under "What your kid does while you're at work", stops just before the hands meet, and rewinds to the morning. The day then plays in order through the updates a parent gets on their phone: outside time, music time, a little helper's high five (the payoff), and pickup. It ends on an invitation to book a tour.

## Meta ad copy

Use both video files in one ad: in Ads Manager, choose **Customize placements** (placement asset customization) and give the 9:16 file to Reels and Stories and the 4:5 file to Feeds.

**Primary text, option A (working parents)**
While you're at work, they're out in the sunshine chasing a ball, dancing in the classroom and high-fiving their teachers. ⭐ Tiny Stars Daycare is now enrolling in Grande Prairie.

📞 Book a tour: (780) 230-1599
📍 10629 West Side Drive, Grande Prairie
🌐 tinystars.ca

**Primary text, option B (the visit)**
Come and see the rooms for yourself. ⭐ Tiny Stars Daycare in Grande Prairie is now enrolling, and we'd love to show you around.

📞 (780) 230-1599 · 🌐 tinystars.ca
📍 10629 West Side Drive, Grande Prairie

**Headlines** (40 characters or fewer)
1. Book a tour at Tiny Stars (25)
2. Now enrolling in Grande Prairie (31)
3. See their day at Tiny Stars (27)

**Description** (30 characters or fewer): 10629 West Side Drive, GP (25)

**Call to action button:** "Call now" if you want phone calls, or "Book now" or "Learn more" pointing to tinystars.ca.

Run options A and B against each other for the first few days. Meta needs roughly 1,000 impressions per ad before the numbers mean much, so don't switch either one off too early.

## Before the ad goes live

- **Parents' permission:** an ad is different from a post. Faces are blurred, but parents can still recognise their own child. Get written permission from the families of the three children shown: the toddler in the snowsuit, the boy dancing and the girl with the water bottles. Ask the teacher whose voice and hands are in the high-five shot too.
- **"Now enrolling":** make sure you have spaces before you pay to promote it.
- **The updates are a dramatisation:** the times on the notifications (9:52 AM and so on) tell the story of one day. They don't claim to be real messages.
- **Privacy:** every face in the film is blurred, kids and staff, tracked frame by frame and checked by eye. The family-photo board on the classroom wall is blurred too.
- **Music:** "Acoustic Upbeat" by OctoSound, from Pixabay, free for commercial use including ads under the Pixabay Content License. A credit is optional: `🎵 Music: "Acoustic Upbeat" by OctoSound (Pixabay)`.
- **Upload quality:** upload from a computer, or copy the file to your phone through Google Drive or a USB cable rather than WhatsApp, which re-compresses video.
